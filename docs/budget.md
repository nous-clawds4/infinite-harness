# Budget

**Status: L3 (disclosed), except where marked.** This note records how the Infinite Harness budgets shared resources across projects. It is maintained by the Chief of Staff (CoS).

## Who budgets

Cross-project budgeting is a CoS duty ([`org-chart.md`](org-chart.md), [`README.md`](../README.md)). The CoS also acts as lead for $P^0$, which has no separate project lead. If a $P^0$ lead is split off later, cross-project budgeting stays with the CoS, because a project lead is a peer of the other leads. A $P^0$ lead would budget only $P^0$'s own share and could propose improvements to how budgeting works.

## Claude Max weekly quota

The weekly quota resets Monday at 8:00 PM ET.

| Line | Rule | Hold |
|---|---|---|
| **85%** | The MacBook Air review runner picks up queued jobs automatically only while weekly usage is under 85%. At or above 85%, Claude asks David before running a job, and he approves or declines case by case. | **L1**: David's ruling (2026-10-11, relayed in chat, not signed). Only David changes it. |
| **95%** | Hard cap on agent use of Claude quota. Agents stop spending quota at 95%, so the rest stays for David. | **L1**: David's ruling (relayed in chat, not signed). Only David changes it. |

Under the 85% line, David has said that quota which would otherwise expire may be spent on autonomous exploratory iteration, even in directions he may not like.

## Grok (SuperGrok Heavy)

The CoS cannot read the Grok usage meter; David reports the percentage now and then. The CoS paces the week around his reports.

## Agent practice (L3, CoS discretion)

- Keep agents lean: batch work and report at milestones rather than step by step.
- The CoS sets and adjusts the day-to-day split of quota and agent time across $P^0$–$P^3$, within the lines above.
